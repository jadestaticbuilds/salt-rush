SALT RUSH

A white desert, a big sky, and wind with opinions. Take the little sail kart. Come back with salt in your teeth.

Drag anywhere to steer, or use arrow keys.
The sail trims itself. Your heading against the wind sets your speed: sideways to it is fastest, straight at it is slowest.
Ride the pale gust streaks for a boost. Thread the orange gates.
Rocks and dust devils cost hull plates. Three plates.
